Quick start
You’re in

Welcome to Muster.

Muster is yours to try. It organises the skills Claude Code has on your Mac: it finds them, shows them in one place, and checks them for duplicates and conflicts.

01Install

Paste it into Terminal on your Mac

 
curl -fsSL https://muster.is/install.sh | bash

On your phone? Open this email on your Mac to install.

Copy it on muster.is
 

Downloads

Muster for your Mac’s chip, about 23 MB

Puts it in

~/.local/bin/muster

Asks for

Nothing. No admin password, no Gatekeeper prompt.

02Start here

The installer ends by showing you what to run next.

 

Start here:

muster opensee your skills on a page in your browser
musterlist them here in the terminal
muster --helpeverything else Muster can do

Fig. 1  The last lines of the install.

 

Then

muster check finds conflicts and duplicates, using your own Claude Code.

Command not found

This window may not know muster yet. Use ~/.local/bin/muster open here. In new windows, plain muster works too.

04What stays on your Mac

All of it. Nothing leaves your Mac.

 

Account

None. There are no Muster servers.

The check

Runs through your own Claude Code, claude -p, on your subscription. No API key.

In your browser

muster open serves the page from 127.0.0.1. It’s never online.
