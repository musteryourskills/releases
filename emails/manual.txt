Muster · Quick start

You’re in

Welcome to Muster.

Muster is yours to try. It organises the skills Claude Code has on your Mac: it finds them, shows them in one place, and checks them for duplicates and conflicts.


01  Install

Paste it into Terminal on your Mac:

curl -fsSL https://muster.is/install.sh | bash

On your phone? Open this email on your Mac to install. Or copy the line from the page: https://muster.is/?variant=manual

Downloads: Muster for your Mac’s chip, about 23 MB
Puts it in: ~/.local/bin/muster
Asks for: Nothing. No admin password, no Gatekeeper prompt.

02  Start here

The installer ends by showing you what to run next:

Start here:
  muster open      see your skills on a page in your browser
  muster           list them here in the terminal
  muster --help    everything else Muster can do

(Fig. 1: the last lines of the install.)

Then: muster check finds conflicts and duplicates, using your own Claude Code.

Command not found: This window may not know muster yet. Use ~/.local/bin/muster open here. In new windows, plain muster works too.


04  What stays on your Mac

All of it. Nothing leaves your Mac.

Account: None. There are no Muster servers.
The check: Runs through your own Claude Code, claude -p, on your subscription. No API key.
In your browser: muster open serves the page from 127.0.0.1. It’s never online.
