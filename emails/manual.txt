01  Install
-----------

Paste it into Terminal on your Mac. It needs macOS and Claude Code.

curl -fsSL https://muster.is/install.sh | bash

Reading this on your phone? Run it on your Mac. The page has a copy button:
https://muster.is/?variant=manual

Downloads: Muster for your Mac’s chip, about 23 MB
Checks: The download against its checksum
Puts it in: ~/.local/bin/muster
PATH: Adds ~/.local/bin to it
Asks for: Nothing. No admin password, no Gatekeeper prompt.


02  Start here
--------------

The installer ends with these three. Run them in Terminal.

muster open
  See your skills on a page in your browser

muster
  List them here in the terminal

muster --help
  Everything else Muster can do

Command not found? This window may not know muster yet. Use ~/.local/bin/muster open here. In new terminal windows, plain muster works too.


03  Commands
------------

A few of the rest. muster --help lists every one.

muster check
  Check for conflicts and duplicates, found by your Claude Code

muster show <skill>
  Show one skill

muster move <skill>… <folder>
  Put skills in a folder. The files don’t move.


04  What stays on your Mac
--------------------------

All of it. Nothing leaves your Mac.

Account: None. There are no Muster servers.
The check: Runs through your own Claude Code, claude -p, on your subscription. No API key.
The page: muster open serves it on 127.0.0.1. It’s never online.
Folders and labels: Muster’s own, kept in ~/.muster/
Skill files: Never move
