Paste this into Terminal on your Mac:

curl -fsSL https://muster.is/install.sh | bash

Copy it on muster.is: https://muster.is/?variant=note

About 23 MB. No admin password. It puts muster in ~/.local/bin.

When the install finishes, run the first command it shows to see your skills on a page in your browser:

muster open

On your phone? Open this email again on your Mac.
