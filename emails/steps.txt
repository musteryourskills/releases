YOU’RE IN

Three steps to sorted skills.

You’re off the waitlist, and Muster is ready for you. It organises the skills Claude Code has on your Mac: it finds them, shows them in one place, and checks them for duplicates and conflicts.

A 23 MB download. No account. Nothing leaves your Mac.


1. Install it with one line

Paste this into Terminal on your Mac:

curl -fsSL https://muster.is/install.sh | bash

It downloads Muster, checks it against its checksum and puts muster in ~/.local/bin. No admin password. macOS, Apple silicon or Intel.

Reading this on your phone? Muster installs on your Mac, so open this email there. This invite is also on the web, with a copy button:
https://muster.is/?variant=steps


2. Sort them in your browser

muster open

Muster puts your skills on one page in your browser. Your Claude Code suggests up to eight folders, then Autosort files the skills into them. The files never move, and the page never goes online.


3. Check them with Claude Code

muster check

Your own Claude Code reads your skills and finds the ones that conflict or repeat each other. It runs on your plan: no API key, no account. Nothing changes until you say so. With a lot of skills, it can take a few minutes.
